# Frequently asked questions

## Accounts

**Who can buy tickets or products?**
Only fan accounts can buy tickets, buy products, or enter lotteries. Staff accounts (managers and
administrators) can't.

**I forgot my password.**
Use "Forgot password" on the login page. You'll receive an email with a link to set a new password.
The link expires after 15 minutes.

## Ticket lotteries

**Does entering a lottery cost anything?**
No. Entering is free. You only pay if you win, after the draw.

**How do I enter a lottery?**
First rank the ticket tiers you want for that concert in order of preference (for example 1st VIP,
2nd Premium, 3rd Regular). You can only apply to the lottery for a tier you've ranked.

**How many times can I enter?**
Each lottery limits how many entries one account can make. Buying albums or merch does not give
you extra entries.

**How does the draw work?**
All tiers for a concert are drawn together, starting with each fan's highest-ranked tier. Winners
are picked at random. If you don't win your first choice, you're still in the draw for your next
ranked tier. You can win at most one ticket per concert.

**How will I know if I won?**
You'll get an email and a notification on the site with your result.

**I won. What do I do now?**
Pay for your ticket before the payment deadline. If the deadline passes without payment, the win
lapses and you lose the ticket.

## Buying tickets directly

**Can I buy tickets without a lottery?**
Some tiers are sold directly, first come first served, during a sale period. They can sell out.

**Can I buy more than one ticket for the same concert?**
No. Each account can hold one ticket per concert.

**Why can't I buy a ticket directly?**
Common reasons: the sale period hasn't started or has ended, the tier is sold out, you already have
a ticket for that concert, or you have a lottery entry for that concert that is still waiting for
its result or that you won. A tier sold only by lottery can't be bought directly.

## Albums and merchandise

**Is there a limit on how many I can buy?**
Yes. You can buy up to 3 of the same product in total, across all your orders. You can buy as many
different products as you like.

**Are prices tax-inclusive?**
Yes. Prices are shown in yen with tax included.

**How do I track my order?**
You'll get an email when your order is placed. You can follow its status in your order history, and
you'll get a notification when it ships.

## Contacting us

**How do I contact support?**
Use the contact form on this page. We reply by email to the address you enter.
